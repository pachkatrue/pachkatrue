# aa22.ru — Production E-commerce Platform

> Production case study for the frontend of [aa22.ru](https://aa22.ru), the e-commerce platform of «Акцент-Авто».

## Overview

[aa22.ru](https://aa22.ru) is a production e-commerce platform for automotive parts, accessories, maintenance products and automotive services.

The public product experience includes:

- product catalog and category navigation;
- search by VIN, vehicle body number, license plate, product name, brand or cross-reference number;
- vehicle-specific parts navigation;
- product pages with pricing and availability;
- stock information across stores;
- delivery information;
- shopping cart and checkout flows;
- customer account functionality;
- automotive service scenarios;
- promotions and content sections.

The production site currently serves customers across multiple locations in Altai Krai and the Altai Republic. The public site also exposes product, catalog, service, promotion and news content. [aa22.ru](https://aa22.ru)

## Complex frontend scenarios

### Vehicle-oriented catalog navigation

The application is not a generic product store. Automotive parts have to be connected with vehicle information, categories, manufacturers and compatible parts.

This creates a more complex navigation model than a traditional e-commerce catalog.

### Faceted filtering

The catalog uses advanced filtering and facets to help users narrow down large product collections.

The frontend is responsible for representing the filter state, communicating it to the API layer and keeping the resulting UI consistent with the selected parameters.

### Multi-step checkout

The checkout contains complex client-side business logic and multiple stages.

The frontend has to keep the state consistent across the flow while coordinating user input, validation, API requests and delivery/order-related information.

### Store availability

Product availability is presented at the store level, including location, quantity and delivery information.

This requires the UI to combine product information with location-specific availability data.
